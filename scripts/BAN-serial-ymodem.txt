BANNED — 2026-09-24 (Nick order)

Do NOT run any `loady` / `sz --ymodem` / ymodem transfer of a kernel, DTB, initrd or any
multi-megabyte file over the board's serial line. Those transfers take 45-70 minutes, hold the
serial port the whole time, and get killed by session restarts.

Deploy path for card files is: stage on host -> scp to tab reader -> human swaps the card.
Sub-250 KB DTBs over serial are acceptable ONLY with Nick's explicit go in the room.

Never power-cycle the board more than once per verified need, and never in a loop.
If the board needs a card swap: page Nick (tell-nick.py), then wait. Do not cycle while waiting.
